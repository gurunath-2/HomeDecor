CUSTOMIZE FLIPKART AFFILIATE LINKS
─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

To add your affiliate links:

1. Open: src/components/Products.jsx

2. Find the products array (lines 8-74)

3. For each product, update the flipkartLink:

   Change:  'https://www.flipkart.com/search?q=...'
   To:      'https://www.flipkart.com/...?affid=YOUR_AFFILIATE_CODE'

Example:
   Before: flipkartLink: 'https://www.flipkart.com/search?q=glass+vase'
   After:  flipkartLink: 'https://www.flipkart.com/search?q=glass+vase?affid=abc123xyz'

4. Each product automatically displays:
   • Product image from /assets/images/
   • Product name
   • Category
   • Description
   • Rating
   • "View on Flipkart" button linking to your affiliate URL
