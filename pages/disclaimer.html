What is an Affiliate Link?

PinsByAish is a participant in the Flipkart Affiliate Program. As such, we may earn affiliate commissions when you click on product links and make a purchase through those links on our site.

When you click a link to a product on Flipkart and purchase through that link, Flipkart may reward us with a small commission. This commission is at no extra cost to you – the price you pay remains exactly the same.

How We Use Affiliate Links

Our affiliate links help support the content creation and maintenance of PinsByAish. The money we earn from affiliate commissions allows us to:

  • Create quality, original product recommendations
  • Maintain and improve our website
  • Continue curating and researching living room decor
  • Keep content free for all our visitors

Product Recommendations & Honesty

We want to be completely transparent: All product recommendations are genuine and based on actual value and quality. We only feature products we believe in and would personally use.

Our recommendations are made independent of affiliate commissions. If a product isn't good, we won't recommend it just because it earns us a commission. Your trust is far more valuable to us than any single affiliate payment.

Identifying Affiliate Links

All affiliate links on Lovely Harbor are clearly marked. Look for labels like:

  • "Check Price on Flipkart" - These are affiliate links
  • "*Affiliate Link" - Indicated in fine print below links
  • Links with fktr.in domain - Flipkart affiliate short URLs

We always disclose our affiliate relationships to maintain complete transparency with our readers.

What This Means for You

✓ You Pay the Same Price

Using our affiliate links does not increase the price of any product for you. You pay exactly what you would pay if you visited Flipkart directly.

✓ You Have a Choice

You are never obligated to use our links. You can always visit Flipkart directly if you prefer. We appreciate your support, but your choice is respected.
